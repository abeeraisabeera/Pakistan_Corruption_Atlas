# Ethics & methodology

## Purpose

Pakistan Public Corruption Atlas is an **OSINT and journalism research** tool. It aggregates **publicly available** information so researchers, journalists, and citizens can explore documented proceedings with full citations.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Do not fabricate** cases, amounts, dates, or attributions.
2. **Do not infer guilt.** Allegation ≠ investigation ≠ charge ≠ conviction.
3. **Do not present allegations as facts.** Prefer verbs like *alleged*, *reported*, *according to*.
4. Every published case needs **≥ 2 independent reputable sources**.
5. Prefer **primary documents**: judgments, AGP paras, Gazette notifications, NAB references, PPRA tenders, ICIJ/OCCRP investigations.
6. Always show the project disclaimer on dashboards and exports.

## Allowed sources

- Government: NAB, ECP, PAC, AGP, PPRA, Gazette, Supreme/High Courts
- International: TI / TI Pakistan, OCCRP, ICIJ, World Bank sanctions, IMF, FATF (where relevant)
- Major Pakistani press: Dawn, The News, Express Tribune, Business Recorder
- International press: Reuters, AP, BBC, Al Jazeera
- Academic / public audit reports

## Forbidden sources

- Social media rumors
- Anonymous blogs / forums
- Unverified propaganda channels
- Private leaks without independent corroboration and legal review

## Confidence scores

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| High | ≥3 independent domains and/or strong primary (court/government) corroboration |
| Medium | ≥2 independent reputable sources |
| Low | Thin corroboration — keep out of “featured” surfaces until improved |

## Display labels

Use procedural labels only:

- Alleged
- Under Investigation
- Charged
- On Trial
- Convicted
- Acquitted
- Case Dismissed
- Pending
- Official Inquiry
- Investigative Journalism Report

## Political neutrality

No party branding, campaign colors as identity, or advocacy framing. Party affiliation appears **only when publicly documented** and relevant to the record.
